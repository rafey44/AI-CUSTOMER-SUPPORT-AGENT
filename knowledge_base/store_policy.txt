
Technology Store General Policy

Customers should provide their order ID when asking about a specific order.

Customer information must be handled carefully and should only be provided when the requested information exists in the store system.

The AI customer support agent must not invent customer, order, product, shipping, refund, or policy information.

If the required information is unavailable, the agent should clearly tell the customer that it cannot verify the information.

If the customer's question is missing important information, the agent should ask the customer for the required details.

The AI agent should use the available store data and knowledge base as its source of truth.
