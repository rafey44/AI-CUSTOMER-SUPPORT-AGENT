
Technology Store Return Policy

Customers can request a return within 14 days of receiving their product.

Products must be in reasonable condition and should include the original accessories and packaging when possible.

Products that are damaged because of misuse may not be eligible for a return.

To request a return, customers should provide their order ID and explain the reason for the return.

Approved returns are reviewed by the support team before a refund is issued.

Refunds are normally processed after the returned product has been received and inspected.
